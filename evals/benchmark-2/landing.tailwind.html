Features

Everything between "start timer" and "payment received"

Lumen replaces the spreadsheet, the invoice template and the sticky note that says "remember to bill for Tuesday."

One-click timers

Start tracking from the menu bar, browser extension or your phone. Forgot to start? Lumen notices idle time and asks what you were working on.

Invoices in seconds

Select a date range, pick a client, and Lumen builds an itemized invoice from your tracked hours. Send it with Stripe or PayPal payment links attached.

Client profitability

See your real hourly rate per client after revisions, calls and scope creep. Find out which projects pay off and which ones quietly drain you.

Budgets and alerts

Set an hour or fee cap for each project. Lumen warns you at 75% and 100%, so you can have the "this is out of scope" talk before it is too late.

Calendar sync

Connect Google or Outlook and turn meetings into time entries with one tap. Your calendar becomes a timesheet you already filled in.

Client portal

Share a private link where clients can see hours, approve timesheets and pay invoices. Fewer "can you send me a breakdown?" emails.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Starter is free with no time limit and no credit card. You can track unlimited hours for up to two clients and send three invoices a month. Upgrade only when you need more.

Can I import my data from Toggl, Harvest or Clockify?

Yes. Upload a CSV export from any of them and Lumen maps clients, projects and entries for you.

Does Lumen work offline?

The desktop and mobile apps keep tracking without a connection and sync when you are back online.

How do clients pay my invoices?

Invoices include Stripe or PayPal payment links, so clients can pay by card or bank transfer in a click.

Can I set different rates for different clients or tasks?

Yes. Set a default rate, then override it per client, per project or per task.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You drop to the free Starter plan and keep everything. You can export all of your data at any time.
