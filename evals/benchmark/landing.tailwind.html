FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the Starter plan really free forever?

Yes. Track unlimited time for one client and send up to three invoices a month at no cost. Upgrade only when you add a second client or need online payments.

Does Lumen take a cut of my payments?

No. Lumen adds zero fees on top of payments. You only pay Stripe's standard processing rates, and payouts go straight to your bank account.

Can I import my data from Toggl, Harvest, or Clockify?

Yes. Our importer brings over clients, projects, rates, and historical time entries from a CSV export in a couple of minutes. Most people are fully moved in under ten.

Does Lumen take screenshots or monitor my activity?

Never. Lumen is built for independent professionals, not surveillance. Idle detection only checks whether your computer is in use, and that signal never leaves your device.

Which currencies and tax setups are supported?

Invoice in 135+ currencies, with per-client currency settings. Add VAT, GST, or sales tax lines, reverse-charge notes for EU clients, and your tax ID on every invoice.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your account drops back to Starter and all your history stays put. You can export everything as CSV or PDF at any time, or permanently delete your account in one step.
