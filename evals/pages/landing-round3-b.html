Features

Everything between "start timer" and "paid"

One-click timers

Start a timer from the menu bar, your phone, or a keyboard shortcut. Forgot to stop it? Lumen notices when you go idle and asks what to keep.

Invoices from your hours

Pick a client and a date range, and Lumen builds the invoice with your rates, taxes, and logo. Send it as a PDF or a payment link.

Effective hourly rate

See what each client really pays per hour once revisions, calls, and email are counted. Fixed-price projects finally make sense.

Calendar import

Connect Google or Outlook and turn client meetings into time entries with a single tap, so calls never go unbilled.

Private by default

No screenshots, no keystroke logging, no activity scores. Your time data is yours, exportable to CSV at any moment.

Budget alerts

Set a cap of hours or dollars per project. Lumen warns you at 80% so you can talk scope before you work for free.

FAQ

Questions freelancers ask us

Is the Solo plan really free?

Yes. Solo has no time limit and no credit card required. You only pay if you need more than three clients or five invoices a month.

Can I import my hours from another tracker?

Lumen imports CSV exports from Toggl, Harvest, and Clockify. Your clients, projects, and rates come across with the entries.

Does Lumen work offline?

The desktop and mobile apps keep tracking without a connection and sync as soon as you are back online.

Which currencies and tax rules do invoices support?

Invoices support over 130 currencies, multiple tax lines, and reverse-charge VAT notes for EU clients.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings and you drop back to Solo at the end of your billing period. Your data stays put.
