FAQ

Questions, answered

Which devices does Lumen run on?

Lumen has apps for macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android, plus a web app. Timers sync between them in real time, so you can start on your laptop and stop on your phone.

Does Lumen take screenshots or track my keystrokes?

No. Lumen only records which app or file is in front, and only to suggest time entries. That data never leaves your device unless you turn on sync.

Can my clients pay invoices online?

Yes. Pro and Studio invoices include a payment link for cards and bank transfers through Stripe. Lumen adds no fee on top of Stripe's.

Can I import my hours from another tracker?

Lumen imports CSV exports from Toggl, Harvest, and Clockify, keeping your clients, projects, and rates.

What happens when my trial ends?

If you don't pick a paid plan, your account moves to Starter. Nothing is deleted; clients beyond the first three are archived until you upgrade.
