Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free forever for up to three active clients. Upgrade only when you need more clients, unlimited invoices or reports.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Your account moves to the Starter plan automatically. You keep all your tracked time and past invoices; nothing is deleted.

Can I import my data from another time tracker?

Lumen imports CSV exports from Toggl, Harvest and Clockify, including clients, projects and historical entries.

Which payment methods can my clients use?

Clients can pay invoices by credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or ACH and SEPA bank transfer. Payouts reach your bank in two business days.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, backed up daily, and stored in the EU or US, your choice. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings in two clicks. Yearly plans are refunded for unused months.
