Features

Everything between "start timer" and "paid"

One-click timers

Start a timer from the menu bar, your browser, or your phone. Forgot to start one? Lumen notices when you open a client's files and asks if you want to log it.

Invoices in seconds

Pick a client and a date range. Lumen builds the invoice from your logged hours, applies your rates, and sends it with Stripe or PayPal payment links attached.

Client profitability

See your effective hourly rate per client and per project, including the unbilled calls and revisions. Find out which retainer is quietly costing you money.

Calendar sync

Connect Google Calendar or Outlook and your client meetings become draft time entries. Approve them in one swipe at the end of the day.

Budgets and alerts

Set an hour or fee cap on a fixed-price project. Lumen warns you at 75% and 90%, so scope creep becomes a conversation instead of a surprise.

Private by default

Lumen never takes screenshots or logs keystrokes. Your data is encrypted, exportable as CSV any time, and deleted for good when you close your account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Solo is free forever for up to three active clients. You only pay when you need more clients, invoicing, or reports.

Does Lumen monitor my screen or keystrokes?

No. Lumen only records the timers you start and the calendar events you approve. It never captures screenshots, keystrokes, or browsing history.

Can I import my history from another time tracker?

You can import CSV exports from Toggl, Harvest, Clockify, and most other trackers. Lumen maps the clients, projects, and rates for you.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export everything as CSV or PDF at any time. After you close your account, we keep your data for 30 days in case you change your mind, then delete it permanently.

Do you offer discounts for students or nonprofits?

Yes. Students and registered nonprofits get 50% off Pro and Studio. Write to us from your school or organization email to apply.
